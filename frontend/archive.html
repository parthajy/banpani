Archive of responses

Every disaster response coordinated on Banpani, past and present. This is a permanent record of what happened, where, and when. Nothing here is ever deleted.

About this page

A response is active while a disaster is happening. When it is over (either the community agrees, or it goes quiet for weeks) it winds down to dormant, then archived. Each response keeps its page as a lasting record.
