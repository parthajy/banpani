The people building Banpani

Banpani is built and run by volunteers, owned by no one. This page shows who is doing what, as public recognition. Right now we are looking for people in outreach, marketing, PR, translation and community verification, not code (the tech is handled in-house). We are not after a big passive list, we want to know the specific thing each person can do.

Want to be on this page? See how to join.

🛠️ Who is doing what

🙌 Open roles - join us

Tell us the specific thing you can do. We would rather have ten people who each own one real task than a hundred names who do nothing. You are recognised for what you actually do.

  • Outreach & distribution - get Banpani to affected communities, local groups and volunteers when a disaster hits. This is our single biggest need.
  • Marketing & PR - social, press, partnerships. Help more people know Banpani exists (within the brand guardrails).
  • Local contacts - if you know NGOs, journalists or community groups in an affected area, that is gold. One introduction can activate a whole region.
  • Translation & native review - improve or add a language (Bengali, Spanish, Hindi, Odia, Assamese, and more).
  • Community verification - verify reports on the live map during an active response.
  • Design & docs - make it clearer and easier to use under stress.

Offer to help - email us Sign up to volunteer
