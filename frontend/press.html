Banpani in the media

Banpani is a free, community-run live map for coordinating disaster relief. During the 2026 Assam floods it was covered by national and regional press as one of the tools helping connect people in need with those able to help.

Featured in Hindustan Times, ThePrint, PTI, EastMojo and Zee News.
