🗺️ Live map

A safe sandbox to see exactly how Banpani coordinates relief for every kind of disaster - before a real one hits. Open any scenario and try it: report a need, drop a pin, mark a hazard, follow the live signals.

Banpani tailors itself to each disaster. A flood needs boats and camps; a cyclone brings wind and storm surge; an industrial leak drifts downwind; a locust swarm follows the wind; an earthquake means collapse and landslides. Each scenario below shows that in action.

🧪 These are simulations for learning, not live emergencies. They never appear on the real India map, the tracker or in search results. In a real disaster, open the live map or report from where you are.
