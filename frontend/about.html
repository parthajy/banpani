About Banpani · বানপানী

Coordinating Community Flood Relief
🗺️ Live map

Banpani is a free, community-run live map to coordinate flood relief in Assam — so no area is left stranded, and no two convoys collide on the same village. It belongs to no one, and it's built by volunteers.

⚠️ Banpani is a community tool, not a replacement for official rescue. Reports are crowd-sourced and may be wrong or incomplete — please don't rely on it 100%. For any emergency, always call 112 / SDRF / the ASDMA control room first.

Why we built it

During the floods, relief is enormous and generous — but a lot of it lives inside scattered WhatsApp groups, where messages pile up and things slip: two convoys reach the same village while another waits, a camp runs out of ORS and the ask gets buried, a stranded family is seen by one person who doesn't know who to tell. Banpani puts it all on one shared, living map so we can see the whole picture at once — together.

How to use it

  • 🆘 Need help? Open the Need tab, drop a pin, say what's needed. No login, no account.
  • 🗺️ Helping? Check the Gaps view — the flooded areas nobody has reached yet.
  • 🚚 Running a convoy? Announce your route so effort isn't doubled — the map warns you if another group is already covering that spot.
  • 🌊 See water rising or receding? Mark it on the Flood tab. The flood map is only as live as we make it.
  • 🏳️ An NGO? List your group so donors and convoys can find you.

How trust works — community consensus

There are no accounts and no gatekeeper. Anyone can report; the community verifies. A report becomes trusted when 3 different people confirm it, and false reports get flagged and hidden. NGOs earn a community-verified badge at 5 endorsements. Victim phone numbers are never shown publicly — they're revealed only to someone actually coordinating help. Every action is visible in the public Activity feed (no names, no numbers, no IP addresses — just anonymous ids), and a public 6-hour report lists everything still unverified and every coverage gap.

Languages

Banpani works in English, অসমীয়া (Assamese) and हिन्दी (Hindi) — switch from the top-right of the map.

Data & sources

Banpani links back to its sources and never claims to replace an official warning.

Map: © OpenStreetMap contributors
Weather outlook: live rainfall forecast from Open-Meteo (auto-updated)
Official affected areas & relief camps: ASDMA bulletins (dated snapshots)
River levels (planned): Central Water Commission — for gauge stations above danger mark
News: aggregated headlines via Google News
Also see: Axom Flood — an independent CWC river-level dashboard for Assam (a great companion to this map)
