About Banpani · বানপানী

Community disaster relief, coordinated
Live map

Banpani is a free website for coordinating disaster relief. It puts needs, offers of help, and hazards on one shared live map, so help reaches the right places and two groups don't cover the same spot while another is missed.

It started in 2026 for the Assam floods. It now works for any disaster, anywhere: floods, fires, storms, earthquakes, disease outbreaks, industrial accidents, and more. There are no accounts and no login. Nobody owns it. Anyone can use it, and anyone can help run it.

Banpani is a community tool, not a replacement for official rescue. Reports come from the public and can be wrong or out of date. In an emergency, call your official emergency number first (112 in India and much of the world, or your local number).

What you can do

  • Report a need. Drop a pin and say what is needed. No login.
  • Offer help. Mark what you can provide: a boat, water, beds, transport, shelter.
  • Mark the ground truth. Blocked roads, shops and clinics that are open or closed, hazard zones, escape routes.
  • See the gaps. Places that have been reported but nobody has reached yet.
  • Add photos as proof. The location data in a photo is removed before it is uploaded.

How trust works

There is no gatekeeper and no authority deciding what is true. The community checks each other's reports. The rule is simple: it takes a few ordinary people agreeing, not an official, to make information trustworthy.

  • A report appears on the map straight away, marked unconfirmed.
  • When 3 different people confirm it, it becomes confirmed.
  • If people flag a report as false, it is hidden.
  • Groups and NGOs get a community-verified mark after 5 people vouch for them.
  • Anyone can flag a fake or duplicate event. A few flags hide it.

Every action is visible in the public Activity log, with no names, no numbers, and no IP addresses, just an anonymous id per person. There is also a public situation report that lists what is still unconfirmed and where the coverage gaps are.

Privacy

  • No accounts, no passwords, no login.
  • Phone numbers are never shown publicly. A number is revealed one at a time, only to someone coordinating help, and every reveal is logged.
  • IP addresses are hashed, never stored or shown in the open.
  • Everything else people add (needs, hazards, photos) is published as open data that anyone can reuse.

See the privacy policy for exactly what is kept and what stays private.

Languages

Banpani works in English, Assamese (অসমীয়া), and Hindi (हिन्दी). It picks your language from your browser, and you can change it from the top of the map. More languages can be added.

Where the data comes from

Map: © OpenStreetMap contributors
Weather: live rainfall forecast from Open-Meteo
Official alerts: GDACS worldwide, and ASDMA for Assam
News: aggregated headlines from Google News
Also useful for Assam: Axom Flood, an independent river-level dashboard

Banpani links back to its sources and does not replace an official warning.

Help build it

If something is broken, or you want to help build, translate, or run Banpani, use the Contact button on the map. No phone numbers are shown to anyone. You can also sign up as a volunteer to be contacted when a disaster hits near you.
