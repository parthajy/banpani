How Banpani works

Banpani is a free, community-run live map that puts needs, offers of help, hazards and the state of the ground in one place, so help reaches the right people and no effort is wasted. Here is what it does for each person in a disaster.

No account. No cost. Owned by no one.

When a disaster strikes, relief is usually generous but disorganised. Requests get buried in messaging groups, two convoys travel to the same village while a third waits, and nobody has the whole picture. Banpani gives everyone one shared, live map, so effort lands where it is actually missing.

🆘 For people affected by a disaster

You can ask for help in under a minute, with no account and no login.

  • Open the map, drop a pin on your location (tap the map or use GPS), and say what is needed: a boat, drinking water, medicine, baby food, shelter.
  • Add roughly how many people are affected, and a phone number if you wish. Your number is never shown publicly, it is passed only to a verifying volunteer, one at a time.
  • Your report appears on the map immediately, so help can find you.

🙋 For volunteers

See the whole picture and put your effort where it counts.

  • Use the Gaps view to find places that have been reported but that nobody has reached yet.
  • Confirm reports you know are true, so the community can trust them.
  • Mark blocked roads, open or closed shops and clinics, hazards and safe escape routes.
  • Sign up once as a standing volunteer so you can be contacted the next time a disaster hits near you.

🏳️ For NGOs & relief groups

Coordinate instead of colliding.

  • List your group so donors and coordinators can find you and send what you need.
  • Announce your convoy's route and destination, so two teams do not stack on one village while another gets nothing.
  • Adopt a need to signal you are handling it; the community confirms when it is delivered.
  • See live coverage across the whole affected area on one screen.

📦 For donors

Give where it is genuinely needed, to people who are genuinely there.

  • See verified collection and drop-off points on the map.
  • See which needs are still unmet and which areas are under-served.
  • Banpani never collects or displays money itself, it points you to the people doing the work.

🏛️ For government & administration

A community-level view that complements official channels.

  • A shared, real-time picture of on-the-ground needs and coverage gaps, alongside official affected-area data and rainfall or river-risk signals.
  • Open data (CC0) that anyone can reuse; Banpani always links back to official sources and never claims to replace an official warning.
  • No cost, no procurement, no lock-in. It is free and open source.

✅ How it stays honest

There is no gatekeeper. The community checks each other's reports: a report shows immediately as unconfirmed, and becomes confirmed when a few different people agree. Fake or duplicate reports can be flagged and hidden. Every action is visible in a public activity log, with no names, no phone numbers and no network addresses, only an anonymous identifier per person. Read more in About and the privacy policy.

▶ Get started

Open the live map to report a need or start helping. Or, to be ready before the next disaster, sign up as a standing volunteer, it takes thirty seconds and your email is kept private.
