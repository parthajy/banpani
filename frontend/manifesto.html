The Banpani Manifesto

A public promise about what Banpani is, and what it will never become.

Free, for everyone, forever. Owned by no one.

Banpani exists for one reason: when disaster strikes, help is usually there, but scattered. We put needs, offers and hazards on one shared map so help reaches the right people and no effort is wasted. That is all it is. These are the promises that keep it that way.

1. Free, forever. Banpani is free to use and always will be. No accounts, no logins, no ads, no paywalls. It costs nothing to report a need or to help.
2. No money, ever. Banpani never collects, holds, or displays money. No donations through us, no fundraising in our name, no payments. Money and vulnerable people are a dangerous mix, and we stay out of it entirely.
3. Owned by no one. Banpani belongs to no company and no single person. The software is open source (MIT) and the community's reports are public domain (CC0). Anyone can read it, improve it, fork it, or run it for the next disaster. It is meant to be given away, not held.
4. Privacy is not negotiable. Phone numbers of people in distress are never shown publicly, revealed one at a time only to a verifying volunteer, and logged. IP addresses are hashed, never stored raw. We ask for as little as possible and never require an account.
5. Honest, not official. Banpani is a community tool, not a replacement for emergency services. Community reports are labelled as community reports. Modelled data (like river-risk forecasts) is labelled as modelled. We always point to the official sources and never pretend to be them.
6. The line we will not cross. If we ever cannot keep Banpani free and true to these promises, we will close it before we compromise it. We will not monetise the vulnerable, sell the data, or hand the project to anyone who would. Free is not a feature here. It is the whole point.
7. An open invitation. Anyone can use Banpani, share it, translate it, fork it, or help run it. It grows by people showing up for their neighbours. If that is you, you are already one of us.

🤝 Be part of it

See who is building Banpani and how to join at banpani.org/contributors. Sign up to help the next time a disaster strikes near you at banpani.org/volunteers.
