Welcome to Banpani 🌊

A free, community map to coordinate flood relief. Report where help is needed, see the areas nobody has reached, and help verify. Here's a 2-minute walkthrough:

▶
  • 🆘 Need help? Open the Need tab, drop a pin, say what's needed. No login.
  • 🗺️ Helping? Check the Gaps view - areas nobody has reached yet.
  • 🌊 See flooding? Use the Flood tab to mark where the water is.

📰 Flood news & updates

Live headlines about the Assam floods, aggregated from many news outlets (Google News). Tap to read the full story.

Contact the maintainers

Banpani is run by the community and owned by no one. There's no call centre. Leave a message here and a maintainer will see it - no phone numbers are shown to anyone.
