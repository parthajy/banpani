# Banpani brand & communications guardrails

For anyone doing marketing, outreach, social posts, or press for Banpani. The goal is simple: help
people find the tool, and never damage the trust that makes it useful. One bad claim is hard to undo,
so when in doubt, ask a maintainer before it goes out.

## What Banpani is (say it like this)

> A free, community-run live map to coordinate disaster relief. Anyone can report a need or find the
> areas nobody has reached. No account, no cost, owned by no one.

- It started with the Assam floods and now covers Odisha, the Barak Valley, Bangladesh, a Colombia
  earthquake response, and a live world disaster map.
- It works for floods, fires, storms, earthquakes and health emergencies.

## The lines you must not cross

- **Never collect or ask for money** in Banpani's name. No donations, no fundraising, no "support us"
  with a payment link. Banpani handles no money, ever. This is the fastest way to destroy trust.
- **Never present Banpani as official.** It is a community tool. Say "community reports, not official;
  for emergencies, call your official number." Do not imply government or agency endorsement.
- **Never impersonate** an official body, a person, or a news outlet.
- **Do not overclaim.** No fake numbers, no "thousands rescued", no invented testimonials. If we
  haven't verified it, we don't say it.
- **Do not publish anyone's private details** (phone numbers, faces of victims, exact locations of
  named individuals).
- **Respect the founder's choice to stay unnamed** on the site and in official messaging. Credit
  "the team behind Banpani" / the community, not a person, unless that person has explicitly agreed.

## Tone

Calm, honest, humane. We are useful, not hype. Under-promise. Lead with the problem (coordination),
not the technology. It is okay, even good, to be transparent about limitations ("reports are
community-verified and may be incomplete").

## Approvals

- Social posts and community replies: go ahead, within these guardrails.
- **Press, partnerships, official statements, anything representing Banpani to an organisation or
  government: get a maintainer's sign-off first.** Reputation is irreversible.
